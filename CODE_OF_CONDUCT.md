# Community code of conduct

Everyone participating in Permesh discussions, issues, reviews, and contributions deserves respectful treatment. Welcome different experience levels, explain technical disagreements with evidence, accept correction, and respect people's time and privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, impersonation, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Critique the work without attacking the person. Do not post credentials or personal access reports.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. They should apply these rules consistently, explain decisions when appropriate, and protect the privacy of affected people.

Report concerns privately to a listed maintainer through an established contact method. No dedicated conduct mailbox is currently designated. If a maintainer is involved, contact another maintainer if one is available, or use the hosting platform's abuse-reporting process. Do not disclose sensitive details publicly to seek attention.

These expectations apply in project spaces and when someone represents the project publicly. This is the project's own policy, not a claim of affiliation with another conduct program.
