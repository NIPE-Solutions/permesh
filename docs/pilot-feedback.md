# Optional pilot feedback

Copy only the questions you want to answer. Keep this document locally unless
you deliberately choose to share a sanitized copy through an agreed channel.
It is not submitted automatically. Do not include tokens, credential references,
real identities, tenant/resource names, raw exports, or private configuration.
Use fictional replacements and approximate timings where appropriate.

- **Build and scope:** What exact CLI/provider evaluation releases or candidate revisions and OS
  did you evaluate? Which kind of review were you trying to complete?
- **Setup:** Did you reach a successful health check and a useful query? Where
  did installation, permissions, trust, approval or credential setup stop you?
- **First useful finding:** Roughly how long did it take? What made the finding
  useful, without identifying the person or resource?
- **Repeated use:** Did you repeat the review after a change? Was the same scope
  reproducible, and could another administrator understand the result?
- **Confusing or incorrect findings:** What did the tool imply, what did an
  independent source check show, and were completeness/limitations visible?
  Can you provide a minimal synthetic reproduction?
- **Identity evidence:** Could you identify the stable account and canonical
  target? Were conflicting or missing observations handled clearly?
- **Maintenance:** How much effort did updates, token expiry, tenant changes,
  mapping review or repeated approval require?
- **Next priority:** What single integration or explanation would most improve
  this workflow? Is an authorized test environment available for qualification?
- **Sharing preference:** If you choose to share this feedback, may maintainers
  ask follow-up questions? Leave this unanswered if you prefer no follow-up.

Feedback does not grant permission to publish names, quotes, logos or results.
Any testimonial or public case study needs separate explicit consent. Report a
suspected vulnerability through the repository's [security process](../SECURITY.md)
instead of including it in general pilot feedback.
